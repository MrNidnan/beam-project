# Beam

Beam shows the current tango, cortina, and next-tanda information on a second
screen, projector, TV, or a browser on your local network.

It is made for milongas and tango events, but it works anywhere you want a clean,
live display of the music that is currently playing.

![Beam in use on a projector or second screen](images/user-manual/beam_0_hero.jpeg)

## What is Beam?

Beam reads the song information from your music player and shows it nicely on a
display for the dancers: artist or orchestra, title, year, the previous song, and
more. You run it on your DJ laptop and send the display to a projector, a TV, a
second monitor, or any phone, tablet, or browser on the same network.

If you can start your music player and use a settings window, you can use Beam.

## Who Beam is for

- Tango DJs
- Milonga and event organizers
- Teachers running classes and practicas
- Anyone who wants an attractive live "now playing" screen without editing code

## Highlights

- **Works with many players** — Foobar2000, VirtualDJ, JRiver, Mixxx, MediaMonkey,
  AIMP, Spotify, iTunes, and a system-wide **Now Playing** source. See
  [Player Support](player-support).
- **Moods** — switch the display style automatically based on the song or playback
  state, with optional timed moods.
- **Backgrounds** — solid colors, single images, slideshows, artist/orchestra
  overlays, and cover art as background.
- **Profiles** — separate setups for different venues, DJs, or event styles.
- **Browser / network display** — show the same screen on a phone, tablet, or any
  device on your Wi-Fi. See [Network Display](network-display).
- **Live display controls** — show a temporary message or black out the screen
  instantly. See [Live Display Controls](live-display-controls).
- **Played history** — auto-save a setlist of the night as text, CSV, and a
  best-effort M3U8 playlist.
- **Log viewer** — watch Beam's log live from `Help > Open Log Viewer`.

## Project status

Beam is a **maintained fork** of the original beam-project. It is kept lightly
maintained with regular fixes and new features. The goal is to keep it working for
real events and, eventually, bring the work back to the main project.
